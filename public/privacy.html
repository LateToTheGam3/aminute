Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

The short version: aminute has no accounts, no sign-up and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share anything that identifies you.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • No account — there is nothing to sign up for.
  • No advertising identifiers, no tracking pixels, no third-party analytics.
  • No location data.
  • No record of which stories you read.
  • We never sell or share data with third parties, because we do not hold any.

What stays on your device

Your preferences are saved in your device's local storage and never leave it:

  • Light or dark mode
  • Whether bionic reading is switched on
  • Whether you have enabled a daily reminder
  • Recently fetched stories, so the app works offline

Deleting the app, or clearing your browser data, erases all of this permanently. We have no copy.

What our server processes

To serve the news, our server briefly handles:

  • Your IP address, held in memory only, purely to apply rate limits so one client cannot overwhelm the service. It is never written to disk and is discarded within a minute.
  • News articles from public RSS feeds. We keep a record of each story we publish — the source feed, the article link and the text we generated — so that we can answer a publisher's query about our own output. These records concern articles, not readers.

Notifications

If you switch on the daily reminder, it is scheduled by your own device. No notification server is involved and we are not told whether you enabled it. You can turn it off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Links to other sites

Every card links to the original article on the publisher's own website. Once you tap through, that publisher's privacy policy applies, not ours. We have no control over and no visibility of what they collect.

Where the news comes from

Stories are summarised from publicly available RSS feeds published by news organisations including the BBC, The Guardian, City AM, Sky News, CNBC, MarketWatch and Al Jazeera. Summaries are written by an automated system from the facts reported, are labelled with their source, and link to the original. We do not reproduce article text or photographs.

Children

aminute is intended for readers aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights to access, correct and erase personal data held about you. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to retrieve or delete. If you believe otherwise, contact us and we will investigate and respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Material changes will be announced in the app.

Contact
